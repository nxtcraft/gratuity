What is Full & Final Settlement (FnF)?

FnF is the complete payout on your last working day. Under Indian labour law it must be settled within 30–45 days. It includes: unpaid salary for days worked in the last month, earned leave encashment (at basic per day, tax-free up to ₹25L under Section 10(10AA)), gratuity (if 5+ years — 15/26 × basic × years), and notice pay adjustments.

Notice Pay Recovery: If you don't serve the full notice, the employer deducts the shortfall at your per-day salary rate. If you serve extra beyond notice, they owe you that many days' pay in lieu.

PF Note: Your PF (12% basic) goes directly to your PF account — it's not part of the cash FnF. After 2+ months of unemployment, you can fully withdraw it via the EPFO portal.

What is included in a full and final settlement

When you leave a job, the employer calculates all amounts owed to you and all amounts you owe, and pays the balance as the full and final (FnF) settlement. It normally includes salary for the days worked in your last month, encashment of unused earned leave, gratuity if you have completed the required service, any bonus or arrears due, and notice pay if applicable. Deductions can include employee PF, professional tax, TDS and recovery for notice period not served.

If you leave without serving the full notice period in your contract, the employer may recover the shortfall, usually calculated as the unserved days multiplied by your daily gross salary. Enter your salary, joining date, last working day, notice period, leave balance and bonus to see a breakdown.

How to use it

  4. Choose the deductions that apply, then press Calculate FnF to see the breakdown.

Good to know

  • Gratuity is payable after five years of continuous service for permanent employees, and the amount tax-free is limited to Rs 20 lakh.
  • Leave encashment on leaving a private-sector job is tax-exempt up to the limit under Section 10(10AA).
  • Most companies settle in 30 to 45 days. Check your appointment letter for the exact timeline.
  • Keep your resignation acceptance, relieving letter and Form 16 together for your records.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the employer take to pay?

It varies by company policy. Many settle in 30 to 45 days after the last working day, so check your appointment letter.

Is the calculation exact?

It is an estimate. Your employer's own policies, such as the definition of salary for leave encashment, can change the final figure.

Is my salary information stored?

No. All calculations are made in your browser.
