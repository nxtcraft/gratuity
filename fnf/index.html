What is Full & Final Settlement (FnF)?

FnF is the complete payout on your last working day. Under Indian labour law it must be settled within 30–45 days. It includes: unpaid salary for days worked in the last month, earned leave encashment (at basic per day, tax-free up to ₹25L under Section 10(10AA)), gratuity (if 5+ years — 15/26 × basic × years), and notice pay adjustments.

Notice Pay Recovery: If you don't serve the full notice, the employer deducts the shortfall at your per-day salary rate. If you serve extra beyond notice, they owe you that many days' pay in lieu.

PF Note: Your PF (12% basic) goes directly to your PF account — it's not part of the cash FnF. After 2+ months of unemployment, you can fully withdraw it via the EPFO portal.
