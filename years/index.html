Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula for gratuity?

Gratuity = last drawn Basic + DA × 15/26 × completed years of service, where a final part-year of 6 months or more counts as a full year. Employers not covered by the Act often use 30 instead of 26.

Who gets gratuity after 1 year?

Fixed-term contract employees, under the Code on Social Security, 2020 (in force since 21 Nov 2025). Permanent employees still need 5 years of continuous service.
