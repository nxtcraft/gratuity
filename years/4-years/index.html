Gratuity for 4 Years of Service — Calculation & Table

Eligibility, formula and gratuity amounts for 4 years of service, updated for the Code on Social Security (2025) and the ₹20 lakh tax-free limit.

Permanent employees: not yet eligible — the Payment of Gratuity Act and the Code on Social Security require 5 years of continuous service (except on death or disability). Some High Courts have counted 4 years and 240 working days in the fifth year as 5 years — if you are close, check with your employer or a labour lawyer.
Fixed-term contract employees: eligible. Since 21 Nov 2025, the Code on Social Security gives fixed-term employees pro-rata gratuity after 1 year, so the amounts below apply to them.

Formula: Gratuity = last drawn (Basic + DA) × 15 / 26 × years of service. For 4 years: Basic + DA × 2.308.

When Does the ₹20 Lakh Tax-Free Limit Kick In?

For private-sector employees, gratuity up to ₹20 lakh over a career is tax-free. With 4 Years of service, you reach that limit at a Basic + DA of about ₹8,66,667/month. Anything above is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate.

New Labour Code Changes (2025)

  • Fixed-term employees get pro-rata gratuity after 1 year instead of 5.
  • Wages: if allowances exceed 50% of total pay, the excess is added to Basic + DA for gratuity — so many people's gratuity goes up.
  • Payment must be made within 30 days of leaving, or interest is payable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much gratuity will I get for 4 Years of service?

Gratuity = Basic + DA × 15/26 × 4. For example, with a last drawn Basic + DA of ₹30,000/month, gratuity for 4 Years is ₹69,231 — payable to fixed-term contract employees; permanent employees need 5 years.

Am I eligible for gratuity after 4 Years?

Permanent employees need 5 years of continuous service, so 4 Years does not qualify unless the exit is due to death or disability. Fixed-term contract employees qualify for pro-rata gratuity after 1 year under the Code on Social Security (in force since 21 Nov 2025).

Is gratuity for 4 Years taxable?

For private-sector employees, gratuity up to ₹20 lakh is tax-free. With 4 Years of service you only exceed that if your Basic + DA is above about ₹8,66,667 a month. Government employees' gratuity is fully exempt.
