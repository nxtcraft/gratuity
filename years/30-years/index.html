Gratuity for 30 Years of Service — Calculation & Table

Eligibility, formula and gratuity amounts for 30 years of service, updated for the Code on Social Security (2025) and the ₹20 lakh tax-free limit.

Eligible. Permanent employees qualify for gratuity after 5 years of continuous service, so 30 years qualifies. Fixed-term contract employees qualify too (after 1 year).

Formula: Gratuity = last drawn (Basic + DA) × 15 / 26 × years of service. For 30 years: Basic + DA × 17.308. A final part-year of 6 months or more counts as a full year, so 30 years 6 months is paid as 31 years.

When Does the ₹20 Lakh Tax-Free Limit Kick In?

For private-sector employees, gratuity up to ₹20 lakh over a career is tax-free. With 30 Years of service, you reach that limit at a Basic + DA of about ₹1,15,556/month. Anything above is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate.

New Labour Code Changes (2025)

  • Fixed-term employees get pro-rata gratuity after 1 year instead of 5.
  • Wages: if allowances exceed 50% of total pay, the excess is added to Basic + DA for gratuity — so many people's gratuity goes up.
  • Payment must be made within 30 days of leaving, or interest is payable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much gratuity will I get for 30 Years of service?

Gratuity = Basic + DA × 15/26 × 30. For example, with a last drawn Basic + DA of ₹30,000/month, gratuity for 30 Years is ₹5,19,231.

Am I eligible for gratuity after 30 Years?

Yes. 30 years meets the 5-year minimum for permanent employees, and fixed-term employees qualify after 1 year.

Is gratuity for 30 Years taxable?

For private-sector employees, gratuity up to ₹20 lakh is tax-free. With 30 Years of service you only exceed that if your Basic + DA is above about ₹1,15,556 a month. Government employees' gratuity is fully exempt.
